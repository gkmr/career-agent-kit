Headline: Head of Product, Developer Platform at Quillstone Cloud | Grew the platform line from $18M to $63M ARR

About:

Developer platforms lose good teams to the same problem: several groups own pieces of the build and nobody owns the wait. That is the problem I work on.

At Quillstone Cloud I run the developer platform as a line of business. We grew it from $18M to $63M ARR and cut median build time 44% in 15 months, through 7 product leads. Before that, at Tidewell Software, I raised trial-to-paid conversion on the CI product from 22% to 41%.

When a team is behind, I cut active projects before I add headcount; focus is usually the bottleneck. I like problems where speed and control look like a trade-off and turn out to be one decision. If you are working on one, I would like to compare notes.
