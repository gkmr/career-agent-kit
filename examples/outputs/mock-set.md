# Mock set: Veridane loop (example output of skills/job-loop-prep)

Eight likely questions for this loop. Each names its playbook row (`references/playbook.md`),
the story from `examples/candidate.md` that fits, and the method sentence that makes the
story repeatable. Hand this file to interview-coach for the mock.

| # | Question | Playbook row: really testing | Story | Method sentence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tell me about yourself | Opener aimed at this team's problem | The wait is the moat | "I treat the time a developer waits as a product with an owner and a P&L." |
| 2 | Should Veridane build or buy hosted compute for builds? | Strategy: investment vs expense | The runners pilot | "When a capability is the same at every vendor, I buy it; when it encodes how our own developers work, I build it." |
| 3 | How would you grow paid adoption without slowing builds? | Product sense: the need under the task | Speed for everyone | "I define an active team first, then measure both goals against that definition." |
| 4 | Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager | Conflict: team vs company, then commit | The enterprise deals | "I escalate when we agree on the facts and still disagree, and I bring options with the trade-off in each." |
| 5 | How do you run a team of product leads? | Leadership across the team life cycle | Stopping work | "I hand over the decision and stay close to the risk until the lead has made two calls I would have made." |
| 6 | What would you do in your first 30 days? | Understand before changing | The wait is the moat | "I learn who owns each step of the build path before I change any of them." |
| 7 | How would you measure the platform? | Metrics: what the team controls vs later value | Conversion at Tidewell | "I count successful builds, not builds, and I add a guardrail for the harm the main metric hides." |
| 8 | What questions do you have for us? | The part the candidate leads | from `jd-crux.md` | "Who owns the wait today, and who would you want to own it?" |

Rehearsal: a 30-second and a 2-minute version of each story, said out loud. The first two
stories also get a 10-minute version. For this VP loop, each core story narrates an outcome
through the org at least once.
