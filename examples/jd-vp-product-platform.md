# VP Product, Developer Platform - Veridane Systems (invented)

Veridane Systems is an invented software company. This job description is written for the
examples.

## About the role

Veridane is consolidating CI, artifact storage and deploy tooling into one developer
platform for self-serve and enterprise customers. You will lead the platform product team
and own how fast a developer gets from commit to a running build.

## What you will do

1. Own the developer platform strategy across self-serve and enterprise customers.
2. Grow paid adoption while cutting the time developers wait on builds.
3. Ensure product, design and engineering operate as one platform team.
4. Partner with security and compliance to ship controls enterprise buyers accept the first time.
5. Own executive communication on platform health for visibility, alignment and accountability.
6. Build and grow a team of product managers and product leads.
7. Define platform metrics and the guardrails that protect developers from slow or flaky builds.
8. Evaluate build, buy and partner options for hosted compute.

## What you bring

- 12+ years in product, 5+ leading product leaders
- Experience with developer tools, CI or cloud infrastructure at scale
- A record of turning a fragmented set of tools into one platform
- Comfort selling to enterprise platform and security teams
